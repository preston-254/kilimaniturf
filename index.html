About the Turf

Experience football at its finest in the heart of Kilimani, Nairobi. Our state-of-the-art 11-aside artificial turf facility is designed to deliver an unparalleled playing experience for players of all levels.

Whether you're organizing league matches, training sessions, corporate team-building games, or competitive tournaments, our premium facility provides the perfect setting. Located in the easily accessible Kilimani area, we're committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and professionalism.
